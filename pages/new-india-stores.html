Client website case study

New India Stores

A focused business site for a Kolkata ship chandling company that needed a trustworthy online presence and a cleaner way to capture inquiries.

Type Business website
Scope Design, build, deploy
Stack Static HTML, CSS, JS

Challenge

Look credible fast without overbuilding.

The client needed a lightweight website that worked well on mobile, clearly explained the business, and did not introduce a complicated admin setup for a small team.

Approach

One strong page with clear structure.

The build focused on strong first impression visuals, direct service explanation, simple contact flow, and a layout that remained clean even on slower devices and smaller screens.

What shipped

  • Responsive one-page site built for mobile-first viewing
  • Business-focused content sections instead of generic portfolio filler
  • Hosting and deployment support for a smooth handoff
  • Simple structure that is easy to update later

Why it matters

  • Lower maintenance than a CMS-heavy setup
  • Faster time to go live for a local business
  • Clearer trust signals for first-time visitors
  • A stronger reference project for future LaunchRoom clients
